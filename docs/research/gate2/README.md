# Gate 2 observed sessions: the kit

Gate 2 asks why people don't get a statement into Fynora. The in-app record shows where each user stops.
It cannot show why: that is what these sessions are for. Someone uses Fynora with their own
statement while we watch, and we note what they do and what they say.

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-gate2a-learn-why-design.md`, §7.

## What is here

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `recruitment-message.md` | Inviting people, one message per group |
| `consent.md` | What to say at the start, and again before any recording |
| `session-guide.md` | Running the session, step by step |
| `notes-template.md` | One copy per session, filled in during and straight after |

## Who runs it

Sid runs each session or is present at it. One person runs it; a second may take notes.

## Where notes go

**Filled notes and recordings never go in this repository, or in any shared folder.**
- This repository is public, and sessions show real bank statements.
- Keep filled notes privately.
- Delete any recording once its notes are written.
- Only the ranked list of barriers, which names no one, comes back into the project.

## After the sessions

Combine the notes into one ranked list of barriers (spec §7):

| Barrier | What we saw | What they said | How often "What stopped you?" answers name it | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- **What we saw and what they said stay separate.** "I didn't know how" can hide "it wasn't worth
  it".
- **Confidence** is High, Medium or Low, and says how many sessions showed the barrier.
- **It is a list of barriers,** not of feature requests.
