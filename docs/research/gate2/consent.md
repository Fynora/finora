# Consent

Say these in your own voice, but keep every point. Get a clear yes each time. Without it, stop.

## At the start

> Thanks for doing this. I'm going to ask you to use Fynora as you normally would, and then to try
> one task. I'm testing the app, not you, so there are no wrong answers, and anything that confuses
> you is our problem to fix.
>
> You'll be using your own statement, so you may see your own financial details. I'll take notes
> on what you do and say, but I won't write down any amounts, names, account numbers or where you
> spent money.
>
> You can stop at any time, skip anything, or decide not to upload a statement. If you do upload one
> and want it gone afterwards, I'll show you how to delete it.
>
> Is that all right with you?

## Before any recording

Ask this separately, even if they already agreed to the session.

> Would it be all right if I recorded this session? It's only so I don't miss anything while taking
> notes. I'll delete the recording as soon as my notes are written, and I won't share it with
> anyone. It's completely fine to say no, and we'll carry on just the same.

If they say no, do not record, and do not ask again.

## Afterwards

- Delete any recording as soon as the notes are written.
- Never share a recording or filled notes with anyone.
- Never put either in this repository or in any shared folder.
