# Session guide

About 30 minutes. The person uses their own phone or computer, whichever they normally use for
Fynora, and their own statement. Run it in person or on a video call with screen sharing.

## Who

Up to 10 real users, at least 5, recruited from the user list with their consent:

| Group | How many |
|---|---:|
| Never uploaded a statement | 4 |
| Tried, and got nothing in | 3 |
| Got data in | 3 |

## Before you start

- Read `consent.md` aloud and get a clear yes. Do not start without it.
- Open a fresh copy of `notes-template.md`. Fill in the session number, group and date. **No name.**
- Do not show them anything in Fynora, and do not mention statements yet.

## Part 1: Unassisted (about 5 minutes)

Say:

> "Use Fynora as you normally would."

Then watch. **Explain nothing,** and answer questions with "What would you do if I weren't here?"

Note:
- what they look for first;
- whether they start an import on their own;
- where they hesitate, go back, or give up.

## Part 2: The task (about 15 minutes)

Say:

> "Find out where your money went last month."

Watch whether they:
- find a statement;
- understand the upload;
- complete it.

**When they hit a problem:**
1. **Record it first,** in the notes, before you say anything: what happened, and what they said.
2. **Then help,** with as little as gets them past it, so you can find the next barrier.

Record two outcomes, separately:
- **Unaided:** how far they got before you helped at all.
- **Assisted:** how far they got with help.

## Part 3: Five questions (about 10 minutes)

Ask these word for word, in this order. Let silences run, and do not suggest answers.

1. Walk me through the last time you opened Fynora.
2. When the app asked for a statement, what did you do next?
3. What went through your mind about uploading a statement here?
4. The last time you checked your money elsewhere, what were you trying to find out?
5. Since then, was there a moment you thought of opening Fynora?

## Privacy, throughout

- **Notes** record what the person did and said. **Never** write amounts, names, account numbers or
  merchants.
- **No screenshots** of statement content.
- **Recording:** only with a second, separate yes (see `consent.md`). Delete the recording once the
  notes are written. Never share it.
- **If they upload a statement** and want it gone afterwards, show them how to delete it in
  Statement History.

## Straight after

- Finish the notes while it is fresh.
- Delete any recording.
- List the barriers in the order they were hit.
